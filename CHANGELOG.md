### Version 1.0.0 - 17 December 2025

#### ✨ New Features
* **Smarter Search:** Finding what you need is now easier. Search is now case-insensitive, so you don't have to worry about exact capitalization.
* **Instant Update Detection:** The app now checks for the latest version as soon as it opens, ensuring you’re always using the most up-to-date features.
* **Enhanced Feedback:** We’ve added new alerts to notify you if specific challenge explanations are temporarily unavailable, keeping you informed at every step.
* **Updated Documentation:** We've overhauled our project guides and overviews to provide clearer instructions and setup help.

#### 🎨 User Experience & Interface
* **Text Selection Enabled:** By popular demand, you can now select and copy text across the entire application.
* **Improved Responsiveness:** We’ve optimized how the app scales to different screen sizes to ensure a fluid experience on both desktop and mobile devices.
* **Visual Consistency:** We refined the borders in our code editor and standardized styling across the platform for a cleaner, more professional look.

#### 🔧 Improvements & Fixes
* **Domain Migration:** We have officially moved our URLs and license validation services from *vercel.app* to **platforcode.app**.
* **Streamlined Bug Reporting:** Our "Report a Bug" link now uses a structured template, helping our team resolve your issues faster.
* **Performance Optimization:** Under-the-hood improvements have been made to increase overall app speed and stability.

#### 🛡️ Security
* Updated internal dependencies and security scanning to keep your data and the platform safe.
* Implemented automated quality checks to ensure every update meets our high performance standards.
